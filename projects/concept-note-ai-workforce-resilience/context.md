## 2. Standing Decisions & Perspective
- **Beyond Monoculture**: The study group rejects both extreme techno-utopianism ("nobody will lose a job") and fatalist doomerism ("5 million IT workers will be fired overnight").
- **The Centaur Thesis**: Entry-level cognitive tasks (L1 code maintenance, routine testing, BPO customer scripts) face rapid compression. Sustainable workforce resilience requires moving from human-hours billing (time-and-materials) to outcome-based "Centaur" units (one human orchestrating multiple domain-specific agents).
- **Domestic AI Services Opportunity**: Even as traditional offshore legacy contracts contract, domestic digitisation (India Stack, vernacular AI integration) presents a massive new absorption frontier.
