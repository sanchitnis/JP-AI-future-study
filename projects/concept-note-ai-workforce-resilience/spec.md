# Specification & Structure: Indian IT/BPO Centaur Workforce Note

## 1. Document Architecture & Outline
- **§1. The Macro Shock: Collapse of the Billable-Hour Pyramid**
  - Why the traditional IT services model (headcount x hourly rate) is dying.
  - Generative code agents and automated testing compressing entry-level task time by 40–70%.
- **§2. The Centaur Paradigm: From Coders to AI Orchestrators**
  - Defining the Centaur model: human domain judgment + agentic speed.
  - The new skill bundle: prompt architecture, context curation, validation, and domain translation.
- **§3. The BPO to Vernacular Knowledge Work Leap**
  - Voice AI replacing tier-1 English call centers.
  - Emerging frontier: vernacular model annotation, local dataset validation, and human-in-the-loop audit for Indian enterprise AI.
- **§4. Macro-Economic Trade-Offs: Job Contraction vs Value Addition**
  - Synthesizing stances from [[ai-impact-on-indian-it-jobs]].
  - How GDP contributions can rise even as headcount growth flattens.
- **§5. The Tripartite Action Plan: Industry, Government, Academia**
  - Industry: Shifting client contracts to outcome-based pricing.
  - Academia: Overhauling undergraduate CS syllabi from syntax memorization to systems design and agent verification.
  - Government: Establishing National AI Reskilling Tax Credits and transition safety nets.

## 2. Reading Level & Tone
- Executive, analytical, balanced, strategic.

## 3. Explicit Non-Goals
- Not a technical programming tutorial.
- Not a labor union manifesto; focuses on economic competitiveness and systemic resilience.
