# Feedback & Next Cycle Trigger: Voice AI FLN Curriculum

## 📬 1. Stakeholder Reception & Impact
- Reviewed by grassroots educators and DIET resource persons in rural Maharashtra and Karnataka.
- Feedback highlighted high excitement for the "Listening Corner" concept, noting that teachers felt empowered rather than threatened by the tool.

---

## ⚠️ 2. Challenges & Questions Identified
1. **Hardware Scarcity**: Many rural classrooms only have a single teacher smartphone with limited battery life.
2. **Background Acoustic Noise**: Multi-grade classrooms often have significant ambient noise that degrades ASR accuracy.

---
