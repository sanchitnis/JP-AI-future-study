# Draft: Vernacular Voice AI for Foundational Literacy & Numeracy

## Introduction: Why Voice is the Frontier of Indian Classroom AI
In multilingual Indian classrooms, literacy does not begin with the written page; it begins with the spoken word. A child’s ability to decode phonemes, articulate syllables, and connect oral sounds to written script forms the bedrock of cognitive development. Yet, in classrooms with student-teacher ratios exceeding 40:1, a teacher can rarely dedicate more than 90 seconds per week of individualized listening time to a struggling reader.

This curriculum provides a 4-week structured framework for educators and curriculum designers to leverage automated voice assessment to diagnose and support early literacy and numeracy under the NIPUN Bharat framework.

---

## 🗓️ Module 1: The FLN Challenge & The Voice-AI Bridge (Week 1)
- **Objective**: Demystify speech recognition technology and establish its alignment with oral reading milestones.
- **Core Topics**:
  1. *The Science of Reading*: Phonemic awareness, oral reading fluency (ORF), and reading comprehension thresholds.
  2. *Anatomy of Speech AI*: Audio feature extraction (spectrograms), acoustic models, and pronunciation scoring algorithms.
  3. *The Indic Context*: Why standard commercial models struggle with regional accents, vernacular code-switching (Hinglish/Marathish), and childhood vocal tract characteristics.
- **Teacher Exercise**: Conduct a manual 1-minute oral reading check with a student, recording audio on a phone, and compare manual scoring with an automated transcript.

---

## 🗓️ Module 2: Hands-on Voice Assessment Toolkits (Week 2)
- **Objective**: Equip educators with practical, zero-cost, open-access tools for vernacular speech evaluation.
- **Core Topics**:
  1. *Tool Evaluation*: Benchmarking tools like [[bhashini]], Google Read Along / Bolo, and offline mobile ASR engines.
  2. *Curating Vernacular Reading Passages*: Crafting graded reading cards in Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, and Kannada with phonetically balanced text.
  3. *Foundational Numeracy by Voice*: Utilizing voice-based prompts for number recognition, mental math, and word problems (e.g. "Tell me: what is 7 plus 8?").
- **Teacher Exercise**: Set up a "Listening Corner" in the classroom where students practice reading aloud into a tablet or shared mobile device.

---

## 🗓️ Module 3: From Formative Diagnostics to Actionable Interventions (Week 3)
- **Objective**: Translate raw automated metrics (Words Correct Per Minute, hesitation pauses) into differentiated classroom instruction.
- **Core Topics**:
  1. *Reading Diagnostic Dashboards*: Identifying specific phonetic stumbling blocks (e.g., retroflex consonants, vowel signs / matras).
  2. *Tiered Grouping*: Forming dynamic peer-learning circles based on oral fluency diagnostics rather than rigid exam grades.
  3. *Gamified Practice*: Designing low-stakes oral recitation games that motivate reluctant speakers.
- **Teacher Exercise**: Analyze a sample cohort's fluency report and design a 3-day targeted remedial plan for the bottom quartile.

---

## 🗓️ Module 4: Ethics, Bias & Preserving Human Empathy (Week 4)
- **Objective**: Establish strict ethical boundaries so technology enhances rather than degrades human-centric pedagogy.
- **Core Topics**:
  1. *Accent & Dialect Fairness*: Ensuring children from non-standard regional dialects or marginalized backgrounds are not penalized by biased acoustic models.
  2. *Student Privacy & Consent*: Strict protocols prohibiting the cloud persistence or commercial exploitation of minor children’s biometric voice recordings.
  3. *The Irreplaceable Teacher*: Emphasizing that AI cannot offer empathetic encouragement, psychological safety, or moral mentorship.
- **Capstone Deliverable**: Participants present a localized 30-day implementation blueprint for their school or cluster.
