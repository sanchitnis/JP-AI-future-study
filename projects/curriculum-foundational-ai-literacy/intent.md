# Intent: Vernacular Voice AI & Foundational Literacy Curriculum

## 1. Problem / Opportunity Statement
India's primary education system serves over 250 million children across 22 major languages and hundreds of local dialects. Despite massive enrolment, ASER (Annual Status of Education Report) data consistently demonstrates significant gaps in Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN). While the government's NIPUN Bharat mission mandates grade-level reading and arithmetic fluency by Grade 3, teachers in multi-grade rural classrooms lack the 1-on-1 time required to listen to each child read aloud.

Recent breakthroughs in on-device, low-latency Automated Speech Recognition (ASR) tailored to Indic accents (e.g. [[bhashini]], AI4Bharat) make real-time oral reading fluency (ORF) assessments feasible. However, primary educators lack training on how to integrate voice AI ethically without deskilling classroom pedagogy.

## 2. Target Audience & Stakeholders
- Primary school headmasters, DIET (District Institute of Education and Training) trainers, and state education resource groups.
- Civil society education non-profits and CSR foundations implementing FLN interventions.

## 3. Definition of "Done"
A modular 4-week workshop curriculum and pedagogical implementation guide specifying:
- Core principles of speech AI for early childhood assessment.
- Practical lab exercises for teachers using open Indic voice tools.
- Formative assessment rubrics and ethical guardrails against algorithmic stigmatization.

## 4. Constraints & Non-Negotiables
- Must assume zero prior coding experience among teacher participants.
- Tools featured must operate on low-cost smartphones and respect regional dialectical variations.
