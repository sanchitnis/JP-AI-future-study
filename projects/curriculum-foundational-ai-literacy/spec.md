# Specification & Structure: Voice AI FLN Curriculum Framework

## 1. Modular Syllabus Structure
- **Module 1: The FLN Challenge & The Voice-AI Bridge (Week 1)**
  - Why oral reading fluency matters in early brain development.
  - The limitations of traditional paper tests and teacher bandwidth.
  - How speech recognition works: acoustic models, phoneme matching, and Indic challenges.
- **Module 2: Hands-on Voice Assessment Toolkits (Week 2)**
  - Using offline and lightweight voice models ([[bhashini]], Google Read Along, open ASR).
  - Designing vernacular oral reading prompts for Hindi, Marathi, and Tamil.
  - Measuring Words Correct Per Minute (WCPM) and expressive prosody.
- **Module 3: Formative Analytics to Classroom Action (Week 3)**
  - Transforming diagnostic audio logs into student learning groups.
  - Targeted micro-interventions for phonetic confusion (matras, conjunct consonants).
  - Avoiding student anxiety and creating gamified, encouraging oral routines.
- **Module 4: Ethics, Bias & Preserving Human Connection (Week 4)**
  - Guarding against dialect discrimination and accent penalties.
  - Data privacy and student audio data retention guardrails.
  - Redefining the teacher's role: mentor, facilitator, and emotional anchor.

## 2. Pedagogical Style & Tone
- Clear, visual, jargon-free, instructional.
- Formatted with concrete classroom roleplays and diagnostic worksheets.

## 3. Scope Boundary
- We do not teach model fine-tuning or Python programming.
- Focus is strictly on pedagogical application and ethical classroom deployment.
