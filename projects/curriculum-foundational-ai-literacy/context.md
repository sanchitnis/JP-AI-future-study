## 2. Standing Decisions & Pedagogical Stance
- **Voice-First Paradigm**: Because 250M+ primary school students in India navigate multilingual vernacular environments before gaining typed keyboard literacy, voice-first AI is the only viable digital bridge.
- **Teacher-in-the-Loop**: AI tools must augment, not displace, the primary school teacher. Automated voice assessments provide formative diagnostic data, leaving classroom emotional rapport and moral pedagogy to humans.
- **Offline Resilience**: Solutions must function on entry-level Android devices in low-connectivity rural school environments without demanding expensive cloud streaming.

## 3. Style and Format
- **Deliverable Type**: 4-Week Educator Workshop & Actionable Curriculum Framework.
- **Voice**: Empathetic, instructional, pedagogical, practical.
