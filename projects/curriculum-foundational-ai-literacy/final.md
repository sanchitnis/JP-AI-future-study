# Vernacular Voice AI for Foundational Literacy & Numeracy: Educator Curriculum Framework (Final)

> **Official Study Group Deliverable**: Prepared by the Education & Talent Working Track of the **JP AI Future Study Group**.  
> **Seminar Foundation**: [[meeting-05-ai-voice-assessment-foundational-numeracy|Session 5: AI Led Voice Assessment for Foundational Numeracy]] (Rohan Katepallewar).

---

## Executive Overview
In multilingual Indian classrooms, literacy does not begin with the written page; it begins with the spoken word. A child’s ability to decode phonemes, articulate syllables, and connect oral sounds to written script forms the bedrock of cognitive development. Yet, in classrooms with student-teacher ratios exceeding 40:1, a teacher can rarely dedicate more than 90 seconds per week of individualized listening time to a struggling reader.

This curriculum provides a 4-week structured framework for primary educators, DIET resource trainers, and school administrators to leverage automated voice assessment to diagnose and support early literacy and numeracy under the NIPUN Bharat mission.

---

## 🗓️ Module 1: The FLN Challenge & The Voice-AI Bridge (Week 1)
- **Core Concept**: Aligning automated speech recognition with early phonological milestones.
- **Topics Covered**:
  1. *The Science of Reading*: Phonemic awareness, oral reading fluency (ORF), and comprehension thresholds.
  2. *Anatomy of Speech AI*: Acoustic modeling, phoneme matching, and pronunciation scoring algorithms.
  3. *Indic Realities*: Acoustic challenges of regional dialects, child voice acoustics, and code-mixing.
- **Teacher Practical**: Conduct a manual 1-minute oral reading test, record audio, and compare manual scoring against automated speech transcripts.

---

## 🗓️ Module 2: Hands-on Voice Assessment Toolkits (Week 2)
- **Core Concept**: Deploying accessible, low-bandwidth tools for vernacular classroom reading and arithmetic.
- **Topics Covered**:
  1. *Tool Landscape*: Utilizing [[bhashini]], open-source Indic ASR tools, and reading companion apps.
  2. *Graded Vernacular Reading Passages*: Developing culturally rooted, phonetically graded reading cards in Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, and Telugu.
  3. *Oral Numeracy Assessment*: Voice-driven mental math checks and verbal word problems (e.g., mental arithmetic, place value verification).
- **Teacher Practical**: Establish a dedicated classroom "Listening & Recitation Corner" utilizing a shared mobile device.

---

## 🗓️ Module 3: Formative Diagnostics to Actionable Interventions (Week 3)
- **Core Concept**: Translating automated oral fluency metrics into differentiated remedial pedagogy.
- **Topics Covered**:
  1. *Diagnostic Analytics*: Identifying recurrent phonetic stumbling blocks (matras, conjunct consonants, elisions).
  2. *Dynamic Peer Circles*: Forming fluid, non-stigmatizing study groups based on diagnostic insights rather than rigid exam rankings.
  3. *Gamified Oral Practice*: Low-stakes choral and individual recitation activities that build speaking confidence.
- **Teacher Practical**: Review sample cohort diagnostics and construct a 3-day targeted remedial plan for struggling readers.

---

## 🗓️ Module 4: Ethics, Bias & Preserving Human Empathy (Week 4)
- **Core Concept**: Establishing strict ethical boundaries, child data protection, and honoring the irreplaceable role of human teachers.
- **Topics Covered**:
  1. *Dialect & Accent Fairness*: Mitigating algorithmic bias against non-standard regional dialects.
  2. *Child Data Protection (DPDP Act Compliance)*: Prohibiting cloud persistence or commercial monetization of student voice prints; enforcing on-device processing.
  3. *The Centaur Educator*: Affirming that AI handles mechanical listening drills so teachers can provide emotional safety, motivation, and ethical mentorship.
- **Capstone Project**: Deliver a 30-day classroom implementation roadmap with measurable NIPUN Bharat learning outcomes.
