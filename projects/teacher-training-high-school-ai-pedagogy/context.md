## 2. Core Problem & Standing Decisions

### A. The "Brain Rot" / Cognitive Deskilling Threat
- When students use commercial LLMs as an "answer oracle" to copy-paste essays, physics derivations, or chemistry balance equations, they experience the **Performance Paradox**: apparent homework success accompanied by severe atrophy of critical reasoning, persistence, and deep memory consolidation.
- Traditional anti-cheating measures (AI detectors) are technically brittle, yield false positives, and foster an adversarial classroom environment.
- **Decision**: Anti-cheating must be achieved through **pedagogical redesign** (in-class oral defenses, process-over-product grading, prompt audit logs) rather than punitive surveillance.

### B. The Socratic AI Coaching Opportunity
- AI can solve Bloom’s 2-Sigma Problem if constrained properly: acting as a 24/7 personal Socratic coach that *guides rather than tells*, asks diagnostic questions, challenges assumptions, and provides personalized scaffolding.
- High school students can be challenged to tackle university-level problem solving, open research, and complex creative projects because AI handles low-level scaffolding while the human teacher provides high-level mentorship and moral grounding.
