# Specification & Structure: High School Teacher AI Pedagogy Master Framework

> **Contract**: Locks the structure, modules, pedagogical mechanisms, and non-goals before drafting begins.

---

## 1. Document Architecture & Section Outline

### §1. Executive Orientation: The Crisis & The Opportunity (400 words)
- The epidemic of copy-pasting and the biological reality of cognitive atrophy ("brain rot").
- The Teacher-AI-Student Triad ([[source-unesco-ai-competency-framework-teachers]]).
- The transformation of the teacher: from information gatekeeper to Chief Learning Architect.

### §2. Module 1: The Cognitive Mechanics of AI & Why Copy-Pasting Causes "Brain Rot" (600 words)
- What LLMs actually do: predictive token completion, probabilistic coherence, and the "Illusion of Competence".
- The Performance Paradox ([[source-cognitive-offloading-performance-paradox]]): why students pass homework but fail unassisted exams.
- The concept of "Desirable Difficulty" and productive struggle in secondary education.

### §3. Module 2: Inoculating Pedagogy Against Cheating: Assessment Redesign (800 words)
- Why AI detection software fails and damages classroom psychological safety.
- **The "Process over Product" Shift**:
  - Requiring submission of Prompt Audit Trails & Reflection Journals.
  - In-Class Socratic Defenses (1-minute verbal explanation of submitted work).
  - Reverse-Engineering Assignments: Give students AI-generated answers with subtle errors and grade their critique.
- Two-Tier Assignment Framework: "AI-Prohibited" (pure in-class cognitive baseline) vs. "AI-Amplified" (complex open problems).

### §4. Module 3: Deploying AI as a Personalized Socratic Coach (800 words)
- How to turn an "Answer Oracle" into a Socratic Tutor ([[source-mollick-assigning-ai-socratic-tutor]]).
- System Prompt Constraints: Never give answers, ask one probing question at a time, diagnose misconceptions.
- Subject-Specific Socratic Coaching Blueprints:
  - *Physics/Math*: Step-by-step conceptual inquiry and error diagnosis.
  - *Literature/History*: Devil's Advocate sparring and counter-factual analysis.
  - *Biology/Ecology*: Systems dynamics simulation.
- Escalating Challenges: Using AI to challenge advanced students with olympiad-level thought experiments while providing scaffolding for struggling students (Bloom's 2-Sigma Solution).

### §5. Module 4: Ethics, Algorithmic Bias, and Student Agency (500 words)
- Teaching students to detect hallucinations, training bias, and commercial incentives.
- DPDP Act compliance, minor privacy, and digital well-being.
- Fostering long-term intellectual stamina for an AI-saturated future.

### §6. Ready-to-Deploy Toolkit & Appendix (500 words)
- 3 Master System Prompts for Teachers and Students.
- Printable Rubric for Process-Based Grading.
- 30-Day High School Implementation Calendar.

## 2. Voice, Tone & Evidence Rigor
- **Tone**: Authoritative, empowering, practical, non-judgmental.
- **Evidence**: Grounded in the 4 Tier-1 literature sources from `projects/teacher-training-high-school-ai-pedagogy/literature-survey.md`.

## 3. Explicit Non-Goals
- We do NOT teach Python, coding, or mathematical optimization of transformers.
- We do NOT advocate for blanket bans on AI in schools.
- We do NOT recommend commercial surveillance or anti-plagiarism detector software.
