# Feedback & Next Cycle Trigger: High School Teacher AI Pedagogy

## 📬 1. Stakeholder Reception & Educator Impact
- Reviewed with secondary science and mathematics educators across CBSE-affiliated schools in Pune and Mumbai.
- **Key Reception**: 
  - Teachers expressed immense relief regarding the "Zone A / Zone B" assessment framework, which eliminates the frustrating and futile cat-and-mouse game of AI detection software.
  - The Socratic system prompt was praised for transforming student mobile phones into active intellectual inquiry tools during evening study.

---

## ⚠️ 2. Challenges & Unresolved Questions
1. **Parental Perception**: Parents often mistake prompt audit requirements for encouraging cheating, needing a clear parent-orientation module.
2. **Vernacular Medium High Schools**: Teachers in state-board regional medium schools requested Socratic prompts pre-tested in Marathi and Hindi.
