# Spec: AI in India — Comprehensive Research Study

   4.4 India's "Strategic Balancer" Position

## 2. Reading Level & Tone

- **Target**: Educated generalist with interest in technology policy (Study Group member profile)
- **Tone**: Analytical, evidence-based, balanced — neither booster nor skeptic
- **Register**: Professional but accessible; avoid dense academic jargon
- **Pronouns**: Third person; avoid first person except in explicit editorial assessments

## 3. Citation Standard

- Every factual claim, statistic, or direct quote MUST link to a `[[source-slug]]` in `wiki/semantic/sources/`
- Comparative claims must cite both sides
- Projections must be attributed to their source organization
- Where data is contested, present the range with attribution

## 4. Data Presentation

- Minimum 4 data tables with source-referenced statistics
- At least 1 comparative analysis table (India vs. global benchmarks)
- Key metrics highlighted in bold at first occurrence
- All currency figures in both INR and approximate USD equivalent

## 5. Explicit Non-Goals (Locked)

- NOT a policy recommendation document
- NOT a startup investment guide
- NOT a technology tutorial
- NOT a comprehensive history (focus window: 2024–2026)
- NOT sector-specific deep dives (surface all sectors at equal depth)

## 6. Quality Criteria

- Every section must contain at least one grounded source citation
- No paragraph should contain claims from more than 2 source categories without explicit cross-reference
- All comparative statements must be symmetrical (equal treatment of both sides)
- Forward-looking statements must be explicitly qualified with "projected," "estimated," or "proposed"
