# Literature Survey: AI in India — Comprehensive Research Study

## Survey Methodology

This literature survey follows the **4-Factor Evaluation Rubric** defined in the [Literature Survey Skill](../../.agents/skills/literature-survey/SKILL.md):

- **Relevance** (30%): How directly the source addresses India's AI ecosystem
- **Depth** (25%): Empirical rigor, data density, analytical sophistication
- **Source Quality** (25%): Authority and credibility of the publishing institution
- **Actionability** (20%): Concrete frameworks, metrics, or deployment models

**Tier Classification**: Tier 1 (≥4.2), Tier 2 (3.2–4.1), Tier 3 (<3.2)

## Tier 1: Foundational Literature (5 sources, Composite ≥ 4.2)

### 1. IndiaAI Mission Implementation Progress (Score: 4.80)
The definitive primary source consolidating official government data on India's central AI program. Provides granular implementation metrics (38,000+ GPUs, ₹10,372 Cr budget, 9.3M GPU hours, 15 empaneled providers) and the seven-pillar mission architecture. Essential for Chapters 3, 5, 6, and 10 of the research paper.

### 2. India AI Governance Guidelines 2025 (Score: 4.80)
The foundational governance framework establishing India's "Strategic Balancer" approach through seven guiding sutras, a three-tier institutional architecture (AIGG, TPEC, AISI), and the "voluntary today, mandatory tomorrow" regulatory philosophy. Essential for Chapters 4 and 10.

### 3. India Semiconductor Mission & Hardware Sovereignty (Score: 4.55)
Comprehensive coverage of India's hardware sovereignty strategy, from Semicon 2.0 (₹1.27L Cr) to the Tata-PSMC Dholera fab and Pax Silica geopolitical alignment. Addresses the critical upstream dependency question. Essential for Chapter 7.

### 4. NITI Aayog: AI for Inclusive Development & Digital ShramSetu (Score: 4.55)
Landmark NITI Aayog study proposing AI as a bridge for 490 million informal workers through voice-first multilingual tools, blockchain identities, and smart contracts. Defines India's unique "AI for All" inclusive deployment model. Essential for Chapter 9.

### 5. Cabinet Approval IndiaAI Mission (Score: 4.30)
The foundational policy document establishing the IndiaAI Mission with ₹10,371.92 crore outlay. Provides the institutional foundation for all subsequent implementation. Essential for Chapter 3.

## Tier 2: Supporting Literature (1 source, Composite 3.2–4.1)

### 6. India AI Defense & National Security Applications (Score: 3.80)
Think tank and industry analysis of India's defense AI landscape (DRDO CAIR, AI Kavach, Operation Sindoor, LAWS position). Limited by classified program constraints but provides important institutional and policy context. Essential for Chapter 8.
