# Context: AI in India — Comprehensive Research Study

## 1. Inspiration & Origin

This project is inspired by **Session 13: AI with Chinese Characteristics** ([[meeting-13-ai-with-chinese-characteristics]]) of the JP AI Future Study Group, which delivered an in-depth examination of China's state-directed AI ecosystem. That session highlighted the critical need for an equally rigorous, data-grounded analysis of **India's own AI landscape** — one that moves beyond marketing rhetoric to examine institutional architectures, real implementation data, strategic vulnerabilities, and the unique "Indian characteristics" that shape the country's AI trajectory.

## 2. Why Now?

India has entered a decisive phase in its AI evolution (2024–2026):
- The **IndiaAI Mission** (₹10,372 crore) has moved from announcement to deployment, with 38,000+ GPUs operational
- Indigenous foundation models (Sarvam AI, BharatGen) have achieved technical and commercial milestones
- The **India AI Governance Guidelines** (Nov 2025) established a distinctive "Seven Sutras" framework
- **Semicon 2.0** (₹1.27 lakh crore) signals hardware sovereignty ambitions
- Geopolitical realignment (**Pax Silica**) positions India as a critical node in global silicon supply chains
- The **informal economy inclusion** agenda (Digital ShramSetu) proposes a uniquely Indian model of AI deployment
