# Intent: AI in India — A Comprehensive Research Study

## 1. Problem / Opportunity

India is executing one of the world's most ambitious AI strategies — spanning sovereign compute infrastructure, indigenous foundation models, regulatory innovation, semiconductor manufacturing, defense applications, and inclusive digital public infrastructure — yet no single, holistic, data-grounded study exists within the Study Group's knowledge base that synthesizes these disparate dimensions into a coherent analytical framework.

**Inspired by Session 13** (AI with Chinese Characteristics), which provided a masterful single-document dissection of China's AI ecosystem, this project aims to produce the equivalent **"AI with Indian Characteristics"** study: a comprehensive research paper that maps what is happening, what the key issues are, what think tanks are deliberating, and what facts and data (with source references) reveal about India's AI trajectory.

## 2. Target Audience & Stakeholders

- **Primary**: JP AI Future Study Group members (176 members across technology, governance, economics, education, and security domains)
- **Secondary**: Indian policymakers, educators, technology leaders seeking a grounded briefing on India's AI landscape
- **Tertiary**: International researchers and think tanks studying India's sovereign AI model

## 3. Deliverable Format

A **comprehensive research study paper** (`final.md`) structured as a ~8,000–12,000 word analytical document with:
- Executive summary
- Seven thematic chapters covering all dimensions of India's AI landscape
- Data tables with source-referenced statistics
- Comparative analysis with global benchmarks (China, US, EU)
- Strategic assessment and forward-looking analysis
- Full citation chain to grounded sources in `wiki/semantic/sources/`

## 4. Definition of "Done"

- [ ] All major dimensions of India's AI ecosystem covered (compute, models, governance, talent, sectors, defense, inclusion)
- [ ] Every factual claim linked to an evaluated source in `wiki/semantic/sources/`
- [ ] Minimum 5 Tier-1 (≥4.2 composite) literature sources evaluated and archived
- [ ] Comparative analysis with at least China, US, and EU
- [ ] Expert review by Study Group domain leads
- [ ] Published as `final.md` with version frontmatter

## 5. Constraints & Non-Goals

### Constraints
- Analysis must be grounded in publicly available data (no classified or proprietary access)
- Focus on the 2024–2026 time window for current-state analysis
- All claims must trace to sources; no speculative assertions without qualification

### Non-Goals
- This is NOT a policy recommendation paper (it is an analytical research study)
- This is NOT a sector-specific deep dive (those are separate project tracks)
- This does NOT cover India's AI history before 2020 except for brief contextual framing
- This does NOT attempt to predict specific future outcomes beyond stated projections
