## 2. Standing Decisions & Policy Position
- **Hybrid Compute Model**: India's national interest is best served by a public-private compute marketplace (10,000+ GPUs subsidized for startups and researchers) rather than a purely state-run monolith.
- **DPI Integration**: Compute access vouchers should be distributed as Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) with transparent allocation to prevent capture by established legacy conglomerates.
- **Vernacular Priority**: Subsidized compute must mandate at least 30% allocation to multilingual Indic tokenization, vernacular datasets, and public-good foundational models ([[bharatgen]]).
