# Specification & Structure: Sovereign AI Compute Brief

## 1. Document Architecture & Outline
- **§1. Executive Summary & The Sovereign Imperative (300 words)**
  - Why compute is the new geopolitical oil and state capacity lever.
  - The ₹10,372 Cr IndiaAI Mission mandate.
- **§2. Diagnostic: India's Compute Bottlenecks & Comparative Deficit (600 words)**
  - Gap against US hyperscalers (Meta/Microsoft > 350k GPUs) and China's state compute clusters ([[india-vs-china-ai-strategy]]).
  - Thermal, energy grid, and hardware access challenges in India.
- **§3. The Public-Private Compute Cloud Architecture (800 words)**
  - Vouchers as Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI).
  - Multi-tier quota: 35% Academic/Open Science, 45% High-Growth Startups, 20% Sovereign Foundational Models ([[bharatgen]]).
  - Clean energy colocation: Solar/wind corridors in Gujarat, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu.
- **§4. Strategic Trade-Off: Frontier Training vs Vernacular Inference (400 words)**
  - Analysis of [[india-frontier-models-vs-applications]].
  - Focus on tokenization efficiency for 22 scheduled languages over brute-force parameter scaling.
- **§5. Five Actionable Policy Recommendations (400 words)**
  - GPU voucher exchange, green compute tariffs, open weight mandate, hardware diversification, and national silicon roadmap.

## 2. Voice, Tone & Evidence Rigor
- Tone: Executive, concise, analytical, policy-viable.
- Non-Goals:
  - We do not assess specific chip architecture comparisons (Nvidia vs AMD vs TPU).
  - We do not critique specific startup products.
