# Intent: India Sovereign AI & Compute Infrastructure Policy Brief

## 1. Problem / Opportunity Statement
India faces an acute GPU compute deficit compared to the US and China. While the Government of India has sanctioned ₹10,372 Crore under the [[indiaai-mission-cabinet-approval-2024|IndiaAI Mission]] to establish a 10,000+ GPU public-private capacity, critical execution bottlenecks remain:
- How will compute vouchers be transparently allocated between commercial startups, public universities, and sovereign initiatives like [[bharatgen]]?
- How can India guard against high energy grid strain and hardware obsolescence (rapid H100/B200 generational turnover)?
- How does compute sovereignty avoid becoming an expensive copycat of Silicon Valley frontier labs?

## 2. Target Audience & Stakeholders
- **Primary**: Policy leaders in MeitY, IndiaAI Mission Directorate, and Department of Science & Technology (DST).
- **Secondary**: Academic AI researchers (IIT Bombay BharatGen consortium, AI4Bharat) and Indian AI startup founders.

## 3. Definition of "Done"
A rigorous, 4-section executive policy brief that:
1. Synthesizes India's compute landscape vs US and China benchmarks.
2. Proposes a merit-based, transparent voucher allocation protocol.
3. Outlines clean-energy data center incentives for tier-2/tier-3 regions.
4. Delivers actionable recommendations for the upcoming FY2026-27 compute procurement cycle.

## 4. Constraints & Non-Negotiables
- Maximum 2,500 words.
- All GPU and financial numbers must cite verified sources ([[indiaai-mission-cabinet-approval-2024]]).
- Must address both foundational model training and application inference economics.
