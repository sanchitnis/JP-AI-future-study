# Feedback & Next Cycle Trigger: Sovereign AI Compute Brief

## 📬 1. Stakeholder Reception & Impact
- Circulated within the JP AI Future Study Group (176 members) and shared informally with academic contacts in the [[bharatgen]] IIT Bombay consortium.
- **Key Reception**: Strong agreement on the compute voucher model as Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI). The recommendation on green energy tariffs was highlighted as especially timely given state electricity board consultations.

---

## ⚠️ 2. Gaps & Unresolved Questions Raised
1. **Inference Latency in Rural Edges**: Reviewers questioned whether the brief sufficiently addressed low-latency edge inference in rural areas with intermittent connectivity.
2. **Semiconductor Geopolitics**: Further questions were raised regarding potential export restrictions on advanced packaging and High Bandwidth Memory (HBM).
