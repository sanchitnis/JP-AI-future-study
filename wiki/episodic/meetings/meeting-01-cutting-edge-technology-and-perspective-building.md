## Executive Abstract
The foundational inaugural seminar of the AI Future Study Group establishes the epistemic baseline and horizon-scanning mental models necessary for interdisciplinary domain experts to navigate the exponential curve of artificial intelligence. Speakers Parag and Saurabh Bodas unpack the speed of architectural breakthroughs, distinguish between media hype and fundamental capability shifts, and provide a multi-dimensional perspective-building framework to evaluate AI's civilizational trajectory with specific relevance to Indian society.

---

## Key Discussion Points & Insights

### 1. Navigating Exponential Technology Curves
- **Linear Intuition vs. Exponential Acceleration**: Human cognition naturally extrapolates historical change linearly, chronically underestimating technological compounding across compute, algorithm efficiency, and dataset synthesis.
- **Horizon Scanning Framework**: Categorizing AI developments into:
  1. *Proven Utility Layer*: Current deployed models (language generation, code completion, vision decoders).
  2. *Near-Term Horizon*: Autonomous agent workflows, reasoning test-time compute, physical robotics.
  3. *Long-Term Speculative*: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), superalignment, civilizational restructuring.

### 2. Multi-Perspective Analytical Framework
- Rather than viewing AI strictly through a Silicon Valley commercial lens, researchers must synthesize four distinct vantage points:
  - **Technical Grounding**: What do the underlying mathematics, training loss curves, and benchmark evaluations actually prove?
  - **Economic Transformation**: Capital reallocation, productivity frontiers, and labor market structural shifts.
  - **Societal & Philosophical**: Human agency, cultural preservation, and epistemological resilience.
  - **National Sovereignty**: Data ownership, compute self-reliance, and avoiding geopolitical subjugation.

### 3. Setting the Study Group Agenda
- Establishing the core objective for the 176-member collective: rigorous, grounded study across diverse disciplines, connecting technical developments to India's ground realities.

---

## Grounded Claims & Technical Dynamics
- **Capability Overhang**: Advanced models often harbor latent reasoning and synthetic capabilities that remain undiscovered until novel prompting, fine-tuning, or scaffolding methodologies are introduced.
- **Cost Deflation**: The inference cost per token decreases by orders of magnitude each year, making frontier capabilities accessible to emerging markets at unprecedented speed.
