# BharatGen Initiative

## Overview
**BharatGen** is India's government-funded sovereign generative artificial intelligence initiative, officially launched under the National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems (NM-ICPS) by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India. Spearheaded by the TIH Foundation for IoT and IoE at IIT Bombay in collaboration with leading academic consortia, BharatGen's core objective is to develop indigenous, multimodal foundational AI models across India's 22 scheduled official languages.

---

## Institutional Consortium
- **Lead Executing Institute**: Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay.
- **Academic Partners**: IIT Madras, IIT Mandi, IIT Hyderabad, IIIT Hyderabad, and IIM Indore.
- **Sponsoring Body**: Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India.
- **Ecosystem Coordination**: Aligned with the ₹10,372 Crore [[indiaai-mission|IndiaAI Mission]] (MeitY) and the [[bhashini|Bhashini]] National Language Translation Mission.

---

## Core Objectives & Strategic Focus
1. **Multilingual Text Foundation Models**: Training specialized autoregressive language models native to Indic scripts, directly addressing the token-inflation penalty imposed by Western commercial tokenizers.
2. **Speech-First Multimodality**: Prioritizing native speech-to-speech architectures capable of handling accented vernacular discourse, rural dialects, and code-mixed speech (e.g. Hinglish, Tanglish).
3. **Computer Vision & Cultural Datasets**: Curating verified image and video datasets reflecting Indian geographic, architectural, agrarian, and cultural diversity.
4. **Public Good Deployment**: Providing pre-trained open weights and specialized fine-tuned checkpoints for public healthcare triage, agrarian advisory services, and vernacular primary education.

---

## Technical Architecture & Challenges
- **Token Efficiency**: Designing customized Indic byte-pair and unigram tokenizers that reduce token fragmentation by up to 60% compared to generic tokenizers.
- **Compute Optimization**: Utilizing distributed data and pipeline parallelism on domestic supercomputing clusters and public-private GPU clouds under the IndiaAI Mission.
