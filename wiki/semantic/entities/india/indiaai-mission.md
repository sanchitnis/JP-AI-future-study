# IndiaAI Mission

## What it is
The **IndiaAI Mission** is a flagship national initiative approved by the Union Cabinet of India under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) with a total outlay of **₹10,371.92 crore (~$1.25 billion)** over five years. It aims to democratize AI compute, build indigenous foundation models, support AI startups, and develop high-quality non-personal public data repositories.

---

## Core Pillars
1. **IndiaAI Compute Capacity**: Public-private partnership building 10,000+ high-end GPUs accessible to domestic startups and researchers.
2. **IndiaAI Innovation Centre (IAIC)**: Dedicated center for indigenous foundation model R&D (including Indic LLMs).
3. **IndiaAI Datasets Platform**: Unified portal for high-quality, non-personal datasets across government ministries.
4. **IndiaAI Application Development Initiative**: Catalyzing sectoral solutions in healthcare, agriculture, and governance.
5. **IndiaAI FutureSkills**: Undergraduate, postgraduate, and PhD fellowships in AI/data science.
6. **IndiaAI Startup Financing**: Concessional financing for early-stage AI ventures.
7. **Safe & Trusted AI**: Frameworks for algorithmic fairness, bias mitigation, and safety guardrails.
