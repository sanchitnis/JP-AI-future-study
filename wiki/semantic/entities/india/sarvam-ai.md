# Sarvam AI

## Overview

Sarvam AI is India's leading full-stack sovereign AI company, building foundation models optimized for India's 22 scheduled languages. It achieved unicorn status in June 2026 after raising $234 million in a Series B round led by HCLTech.

## Key Facts

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Founded** | 2023 |
| **Headquarters** | Bengaluru, India |
| **Unicorn Status** | June 2026 |
| **Series B** | $234 million (part of $300M round) |
| **Lead Investor** | HCLTech |
| **Focus** | Full-stack sovereign AI: speech, vision, text |
| **Language Coverage** | 22 scheduled Indian languages |
| **Deployment** | Enterprise + government services |

## Strategic Significance

- Selected under **IndiaAI Mission** for indigenous foundation model development
- Represents the **venture-backed commercial model** (vs. BharatGen's academic model and Krutrim's corporate model)
- Active deployment across government services, banking, agriculture, and defense
- Prioritizes "efficiency-first" architectures (MoE) for India's price-sensitive market
