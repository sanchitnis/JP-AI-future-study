## 🔍 Key Extracted Claims & Regulatory Mandates

1. **Compulsory AI Literacy by 2027–28**: Following pilot skill electives in Class 9 and 11, AI and Computational Thinking will become mandatory foundational literacies across secondary education.
2. **Cross-Disciplinary Infusion**: AI is strictly prohibited from being treated as an isolated computer-science silo. Teachers across sciences, humanities, languages, and commerce must incorporate AI modeling, ethical debates, and data analysis into daily pedagogy.
3. **Teacher-Centric Capacity Building**: Successful classroom deployment depends entirely on teacher confidence. National capacity initiatives require high school educators to move from passive consumers to creative curators of AI-scaffolded inquiry.
