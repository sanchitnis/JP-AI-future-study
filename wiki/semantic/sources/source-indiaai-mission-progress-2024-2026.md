## Key Extracted Claims

1. **₹10,371.92 crore** (~$1.25B) allocated over 5 years (2024–2029) for the IndiaAI Mission
2. **38,000+ GPUs** onboarded across 15 empaneled providers (as of September 2026)
3. **9.3 million GPU hours** sanctioned across hundreds of approved projects
4. **1.1 EFLOPS** High-Performance AI Compute System under construction at NIC, Delhi
5. **40% subsidy** on compute costs for eligible startups and researchers
6. **Seven-pillar architecture**: Compute, Foundation Models, AIKosh, Applications, FutureSkills, Startup Finance, Safe AI
7. **BharatGen**: ₹988.6 crore for IIT Bombay-led multimodal foundation model consortium
8. **Sarvam AI**: Unicorn status (June 2026), $234M Series B — India's leading sovereign AI company
9. **$1.3 billion** raised by Indian AI startups in 2025 (3x YoY increase)
10. **$500–600 billion** projected AI contribution to India's GDP by 2030 (NITI Aayog)
