## 🔍 Key Extracted Claims & Cautionary Lessons

1. **The Backlash Against Screen Replacement**: In March 2025, South Korea attempted to mandate AI Digital Textbooks across mathematics, English, and informatics. Widespread resistance erupted from parents and teachers regarding excessive screen time, loss of physical handwriting stamina, digital fatigue, and the erosion of classroom relational depth.
2. **The Legislative Demotion (August 2025)**: In response to nationwide public opposition, the South Korean National Assembly stripped AI digital textbooks of their "official core textbook" status, reclassifying them as **optional supplementary materials** under individual school principal discretion.
3. **The Hybrid Mandate**: Attempting to replace teachers or traditional textbooks with algorithmic tutoring triggers fierce societal rejection. The only viable path forward is a **deliberate hybrid model**: preserving physical handwriting, book reading, and human Socratic inquiry for core concepts, while deploying AI strictly as an auxiliary diagnostic tool.
