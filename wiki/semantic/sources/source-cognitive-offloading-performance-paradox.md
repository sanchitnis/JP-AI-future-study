## 🔍 Key Extracted Claims & Grounded Findings

1. **The Performance Paradox**: Students who use generative AI as an "answer oracle" complete practice tasks 30–50% faster, but experience a sharp drop in retention during unassisted testing. Fast output is disconnected from neural encoding.
2. **The Illusion of Competence**: Fluent, well-structured LLM answers induce cognitive ease, leading students to confuse reading an AI solution with developing genuine problem-solving mastery.
3. **Cognitive Stunting**: Chronic reliance on AI to formulate ideas, write opening paragraphs, or solve algebraic proofs atrophies the mental stamina required for independent analytical thought.
4. **Pedagogical Antidote (Desirable Difficulty)**: Teachers must structure assignments so that students must first hypothesize, struggle, and document their raw thought process before using AI to challenge or refine their draft.
