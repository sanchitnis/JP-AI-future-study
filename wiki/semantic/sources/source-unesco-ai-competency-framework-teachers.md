## 🔍 Key Extracted Claims & Grounded Principles

1. **The Teacher-AI-Student Triad**: AI fundamentally alters classroom dynamics from a dyad (teacher-student) to a triad. The human teacher must remain the pedagogical orchestrator, ensuring AI acts as a scaffold rather than an autonomous authority.
2. **Five Competency Spheres**:
   - *Human-Centred Mindset*: Prioritizing human agency and social development over algorithmic convenience.
   - *Ethics of AI*: Addressing algorithmic bias, privacy, and carbon footprints.
   - *AI Foundations*: Understanding probabilistic generation and hallucinations to prevent blind faith.
   - *AI Pedagogy*: Designing active inquiry, collaborative problem-solving, and differentiated coaching.
   - *Professional Learning*: Using AI for lesson planning to unlock hours for student mentorship.
3. **Guardrails for High School Learners**: AI must never be permitted to bypass childhood critical thinking; ethical use mandates transparency, student data protection, and deliberate human oversight.
