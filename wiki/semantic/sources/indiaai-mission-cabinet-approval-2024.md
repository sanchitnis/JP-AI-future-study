# Cabinet Approval of ₹10,372 Crore IndiaAI Mission (March 2024)

**Type**: Policy Document / Government Gazette Press Release  
**Publisher**: Press Information Bureau (PIB), Government of India / MeitY  
**Date**: 2024-03-07  
**Official Reference**: PIB Release ID 2012351  

---

## Executive Summary
The Union Cabinet chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi approved the comprehensive **IndiaAI Mission** with an outlay of **₹10,371.92 crore** over 5 years. The program establishes an AI supercomputing cloud of 10,000+ GPUs, funds indigenous multimodal and Indic foundation models, creates open data repositories, and supports startups and talent development.

---

## Key Extracted Claims & Metrics
- **Budget Outlay**: ₹10,371.92 Crore (~$1.25 billion USD).
- **Target Compute**: 10,000 or more GPUs procured via public-private partnerships (PPP).
- **Startups & Research**: Subsidized compute credits allocated through a competitive bidding model.
- **Multilingual R&D**: Focus on indigenous multimodal models capable of processing 22 scheduled Indian languages.

---

## Implications for India
- Establishes the foundational public compute layer needed to mitigate dependence on foreign hyperscalers.
- Connects grassroots academic research directly to compute resources previously unavailable domestically.
