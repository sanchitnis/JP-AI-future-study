## Key Extracted Claims

1. **Seven Guiding Sutras**: Trust, People-First, Innovation over Restraint, Fairness & Equity, Safety/Resilience/Sustainability, Accountability, Transparency
2. **Three-tier institutional architecture**: AIGG (policy), TPEC (technical), AISI (safety testing)
3. **IT Rules Amendment (Feb 2026)**: Statutory definition of SGI; 2–3 hour deepfake takedown mandate
4. **DPDP Act 2023**: Foundation for AI data accountability
5. **RBI FREE-AI (Aug 2025)**: First sectoral AI ethics framework for financial services
6. **Philosophy**: "Voluntary today, mandatory tomorrow" — designed to evolve
7. **Strategic position**: "Innovation over Restraint" — explicitly pro-innovation vs. EU precautionary approach
