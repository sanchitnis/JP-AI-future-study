## 🔍 Key Extracted Claims & Systemic Rules

1. **The Socratic Constraint**: Default LLM behavior (providing instant answers) destroys student curiosity. Imposing system prompt constraints—*never give answers, ask one question at a time, demand justification, diagnose root misconceptions*—forces students to remain the active cognitive agent.
2. **Addressing Bloom's 2-Sigma Problem**: Individualized 1-on-1 human tutoring shifts student performance upward by 2 standard deviations, but is cost-prohibitive in standard schools. A properly constrained Socratic AI provides high-frequency, responsive dialogue at zero marginal cost.
3. **Scaffolding for Higher Challenges**: By offloading diagnostic tracking and customized difficulty scaling to the Socratic AI coach, high school teachers can challenge advanced students with university-level problem sets while simultaneously giving remedial scaffolding to struggling students.
