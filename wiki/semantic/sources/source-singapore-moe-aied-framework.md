## 🔍 Key Extracted Claims & Grounded Principles

1. **Walled Garden vs. Open Consumer AI**: Singapore rejects unmoderated student use of commercial chatbots. Instead, AI services are integrated into the national **Student Learning Space (SLS)**, shielded by GovTech safety tools (*Litmus* and *Sentinel*) that prevent data leakage and filter inappropriate outputs.
2. **The Socratic Learning Assistant (LEA)**: Deployed for students in Primary 4 and above, LEA is architecturally constrained to **refuse to give direct answers**. It asks diagnostic sub-questions, encourages students to articulate step-by-step reasoning, and prompts self-correction.
3. **Teacher-in-the-Loop Augmentation**:
   - *Authoring Copilot (ACP)*: Generates differentiated lesson materials aligned with Singapore syllabi.
   - *Data Assistant (DAT)*: Scans open-ended student responses to group common misconceptions, enabling teachers to intervene with targeted small-group coaching.
   - *Feedback Assistants (AFA/SAFA)*: AI drafts formative feedback comments, but human teachers retain full authority to review, edit, or reject them before release.
4. **Age-Progressive Safeguards**: AI tools are withheld from early primary students (P1–P3) to preserve foundational brain development, fine motor skills, and face-to-face socio-emotional learning.
