## 🔍 Key Extracted Claims & Grounded Principles

1. **The Four-Dimensional Spiral Curriculum (认知, 技能, 思维, 价值观)**:
   - *Primary (Grades 1–6)*: Algorithmic intuition and physical robotics. **Strict ban on independent student use of open generative AI text tools** to protect emerging reading and reasoning faculties.
   - *Junior High (Grades 7–9)*: Core ML principles, algorithmic bias, and ethical digital citizenship.
   - *Senior High (Grades 10–12)*: **Systems Thinking (系统思维)** and multidisciplinary problem-solving. Students build machine learning models applied to physics, ecological systems, and social dynamics.
2. **Anti-Substitution & Anti-Cognitive Surrender Rules**:
   - *Teachers*: Explicitly prohibited from using AI to automate core mentoring, moral education, or emotional engagement.
   - *Students*: Banned from submitting AI-generated prose or code without demonstrative personal mastery and original conceptual justification.
3. **Mandatory National Baseline**: Requires a minimum of 8 instructional hours dedicated to AI literacy per year across all schools, embedded into the national *Education Powerhouse 2035* roadmap.
