# Compute Capacity, GPUs & Energy Infrastructure in India

## Overview
AI compute capacity has emerged as the defining strategic bottleneck for national artificial intelligence development. India's strategy balances public procurement of high-end GPU clusters (via the [[indiaai-mission|IndiaAI Mission]]) with private data center expansion powered by renewable energy corridors.

## Core Infrastructure Pillars
1. **Public AI Compute Cloud**: Provisioning 10,000+ GPUs to Indian startups, researchers, and academic institutions at subsidized access tiers.
2. **Energy & Data Center Colocation**: Aligning high-density compute facilities with green power corridors (solar/wind in Western and Southern India).
3. **Semiconductor Integration**: Connecting downstream GPU usage with long-term domestic fab and packaging initiatives under the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM).
