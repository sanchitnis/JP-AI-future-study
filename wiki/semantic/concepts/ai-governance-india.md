# India AI Governance & Seven Sutras Framework

## Overview

India's AI governance model is anchored in the **India AI Governance Guidelines** released by MeitY in November 2025. The framework adopts a distinctive "light-touch," principle-based, risk-proportional approach — deliberately rejecting both the EU's prescriptive regulatory model and China's state-directed content control.

## The Seven Guiding Sutras

1. **Trust as the Foundation** — AI systems must foster public confidence
2. **People-First** — Human dignity, rights, and welfare above all
3. **Innovation over Restraint** — Regulatory frameworks must enable innovation
4. **Fairness & Equity** — Address and mitigate societal biases
5. **Safety, Resilience & Sustainability** — Robust, secure, environmentally conscious
6. **Accountability** — Clear chains of responsibility for AI outcomes
7. **Transparency** — Explainable and auditable decision-making

## Institutional Architecture

- **AI Governance Group (AIGG)**: Inter-ministerial policy coordination
- **Technology & Policy Expert Committee (TPEC)**: Technical/legal advisory
- **AI Safety Institute (AISI)**: Safety testing, risk evaluation, benchmarking

## Regulatory Instruments

- **IT Rules Amendment (Feb 2026)**: SGI definition, deepfake takedowns, traceability mandates
- **DPDP Act 2023**: Data privacy and algorithmic processing accountability
- **Sectoral Regulators**: RBI FREE-AI (finance), SEBI (capital markets), TRAI (telecom)
- **Digital India Act**: Anticipated comprehensive legislation

## Strategic Position: "Strategic Balancer"

India's approach is characterized as:
- **Innovation-permissive**: Explicitly prioritizes growth over caution
- **Evolutionary**: "Voluntary today, mandatory tomorrow"
- **Sectoral**: Leverages existing domain regulators
- **DPI-integrated**: Layered on India Stack infrastructure
