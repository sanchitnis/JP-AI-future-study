# India Semiconductor & Hardware Sovereignty

## Overview

India's semiconductor strategy represents a long-term bet on hardware sovereignty, moving from near-total import dependence toward domestic manufacturing capability. The strategy spans fab manufacturing, assembly/packaging, equipment, and geopolitical supply chain diversification.

## India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)

### ISM 1.0 (2021)
- Original outlay: ₹76,000 crore
- Focus: Attracting foreign fab investment, ATMP facilities

### Semicon 2.0 (July 2026)
- **Expanded outlay**: ₹1.27 lakh crore
- **New scope**: Equipment, materials, advanced packaging, indigenous IP
- **12 semiconductor projects** approved across India

## Geopolitical Positioning

### Pax Silica Coalition
- US-led global silicon supply chain security initiative
- India joined alongside Japan, Netherlands, South Korea
- Goal: Diversify from Taiwan/TSMC concentration risk

### Strategic Multi-Alignment
- Western hardware access (Nvidia, AMD, Intel) + Chinese open-weights utilization
- Data sovereignty mandates (DPDP Act, RBI localization)
