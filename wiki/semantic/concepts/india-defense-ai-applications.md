# India Defense AI Applications

## Overview

India's defense AI architecture integrates DRDO's research capabilities with private sector innovation through structured programs (iDEX, ADITI). The approach prioritizes indigenous development under the Atmanirbhar Bharat framework while maintaining human-in-the-loop principles for autonomous systems.

## Institutional Architecture

- **DRDO CAIR**: Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics
- **DAIC**: Defence AI Council (strategic oversight)
- **DAIPA**: Defence AI Project Agency (implementation)
- **iDEX**: Innovations for Defence Excellence (startup integration)
- **ADITI**: Advanced technology incubation

## Core Capabilities

### Cybersecurity
- AI Kavach: Predictive threat intelligence
- Indigenous LLMs for vulnerability discovery
- Shift from reactive to anticipatory cyber defense

### Autonomous Systems
- D4 Anti-Drone System
- Intelligent border surveillance (sensor-radar-AI fusion)
- Autonomous patrol platforms

### Intelligence (C4ISR)
- Satellite data processing, target detection, deepfake detection
- Multi-sensor data fusion (deployed in Operation Sindoor, May 2025)

## LAWS Position
- Human-in-the-loop commitment
- IHL sufficiency for regulation (India's CCW position)
- Active engagement in international forums
