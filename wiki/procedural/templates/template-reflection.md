# Process Reflection: [Period / Year]

> **The Process Loop**: A periodic, slow-cadence audit of the entire workflow across all study group projects, testing whether the AI-native machinery is actually earning its keep.

---

## 1. Where did stages actually earn their keep?
- *Did `spec.md` prevent rewrites or merely add bureaucratic delay?*
- *Did continuous review in `review.md` catch critical issues?*

## 2. Where is human attention actually going?
- *Are domain experts still reading every word start-to-finish?*
- *Has human judgment successfully shifted to high-leverage framing, ethics, and strategic alignment?*

## 3. Are certified skills drifting from active standards?
- *Which skills need re-certification due to policy shifts, institutional changes, or new research?*
- *Which repeated corrections are ready to graduate into new certified skills?*

## 4. Is the loop shape still right for this work?
- *Which projects need lightweight loops vs. full 6-stage machinery?*
- *What workflow adjustments should be adopted for the upcoming cycle?*
