## 🎙️ NotebookLM Audio Overview Blueprint (Podcast Prompt)

```text
Host an incisive geopolitical and technological debate on India's position in the global AI race.
Explore the central argument: India is not following the US venture-frontier model or China's
state-champion approach, but is pioneering a "DPI-first, inclusion-oriented, regulation-light" path.
Discuss the reality behind the numbers: ₹10,372 crore budget, 38,000 empaneled GPUs, BharatGen,
and Sarvam AI. Address hard critical questions: Can India compete without massive frontier compute
clusters? What happens to 5 million Indian IT and BPO workers as code and voice are automated?
How will the India Semiconductor Mission achieve genuine hardware sovereignty? Maintain a balanced,
authoritative, and lively dialogue.
```

## ❓ Probing Policy Questions to Ask the Notebook

1. *What makes India's "Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) approach" to AI structurally different from the US and EU models?*
2. *How is the IndiaAI Mission subsidizing compute for startups, and is 38,000 GPUs sufficient for sovereign AI development?*
3. *What are the "Seven Sutras" of Indian AI governance, and how do they balance innovation with deepfake liability?*
4. *How vulnerable is India's IT services export economy to generative AI automation over the next 3–5 years?*
