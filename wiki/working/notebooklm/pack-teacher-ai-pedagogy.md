## 🎙️ NotebookLM Audio Overview Blueprint (Podcast Prompt)

Use the following prompt in NotebookLM's customization box or chat interface to guide the Deep Dive discussion:

```text
Focus the conversation on the central paradox facing high school teachers today:
Students who use unconstrained AI to do their homework get high marks in the short term,
but suffer from "brain rot" and cognitive deskilling because they bypass the mental friction
of learning. Contrast this with the Socratic AI model—where AI is programmed to refuse giving
direct answers and instead acts as a personalized coach. Highlight practical solutions:
Mollick's 7 AI roles, Singapore's walled-garden SLS system, China's neural protection rules
for younger students, and how teachers should flip homework to in-class oral defenses.
Maintain an engaging, urgent tone suitable for secondary school educators and parents.
```

## ❓ Probing Study Questions to Ask the Notebook

1. *What is the "Performance Paradox" in secondary student AI usage, and why do unassisted test scores collapse when AI is removed?*
2. *How does Mollick's Socratic Tutoring prompt differ fundamentally from standard conversational prompts?*
3. *What specific lessons should Indian school boards (CBSE/ICSE) adopt from Singapore's Student Learning Space (SLS)?*
4. *How can a humanities teacher prevent student essays from becoming homogeneous LLM output without relying on unreliable AI detectors?*
