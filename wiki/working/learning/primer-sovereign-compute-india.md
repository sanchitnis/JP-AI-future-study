# Executive Primer: Sovereign Compute & AI Infrastructure in India

**Target Level**: Policy Practitioner / Senior Executive  
**Reading Time**: 10 minutes  
**Prerequisites**: Basic familiarity with AI and cloud infrastructure.

---

## 1. Executive Summary: Why Compute is the New Oil

Compute (measured in high-performance GPU clusters, memory bandwidth, and interconnects) is the physical substrate upon which modern artificial intelligence is built. In the 20th century, industrial sovereignty depended on steel, electricity, and telecommunications. In the 21st century, cognitive sovereignty depends on **access to compute capacity**.

For India, compute presents a dual challenge:
1. **Geopolitical Dependency**: Advanced GPU silicon (Nvidia, AMD, TSMC fab capacity) is concentrated overseas.
2. **Capital Barriers**: Frontier training clusters cost hundreds of millions of dollars, creating an entry barrier for Indian researchers and startups.

---

## 2. India's Policy Response: The IndiaAI Compute Pillar

Under the ₹10,372 Crore [[indiaai-mission|IndiaAI Mission]], the Government of India established a public-private partnership model:
- **10,000+ GPU Compute Facility**: Procured through competitive bidding with private cloud service providers and data center operators.
- **Tiered Subsidies**: Subsidized compute credits allocated to domestic startups, premier academic institutes (IITs/IISc), and public interest research.
- **National Data Platform**: Safe harbor data repositories connected directly to high-throughput compute clusters.

---

## 3. Socratic Reflection & Discussion Prompts for Working Groups

1. **Resource Allocation**: If demand for subsidized GPUs exceeds supply by 10x, should compute be prioritized for commercial SaaS startups generating foreign revenue, or academic research tackling low-resource Indian languages?
2. **Energy & Carbon Tradeoffs**: High-density AI data centers require 50–100MW of continuous power. How does India reconcile expanding AI compute with national renewable energy targets?
3. **Silicon Independence vs Speed**: Should India wait for long-term domestic AI chip designs to mature, or double down on global procurement to maintain short-term application momentum?
