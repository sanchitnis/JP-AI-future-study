## Key Facts & Data Points

### Budget & Governance
- **Total Outlay**: ₹10,371.92 crore (~$1.25 billion USD) over 5 years (2024–2029)
- **Implementing Body**: IndiaAI Independent Business Division (IBD) under MeitY
- **Strategic Oversight**: NITI Aayog Output-Outcome Monitoring Framework (OOMF)
- **Approach**: "Make AI in India" and "Make AI for India"

### Compute Infrastructure (as of September 2026)
- **GPUs Onboarded**: 38,000+ GPUs across empaneled compute service providers
- **Empaneled Providers**: 15 AI compute service providers
- **Hardware Diversity**: NVIDIA H100, H200, A100; AMD MI300X; Intel Gaudi 2
- **Subsidy Rate**: Up to 40% reduced costs for eligible users
- **GPU Hours Sanctioned**: Over 93.18 lakh (9.3 million) GPU hours across hundreds of approved projects
- **High-Performance System**: ~1.1 EFLOPS capacity being established at NIC Data Centre, Shastri Park, Delhi

### Seven Pillars of IndiaAI Mission
1. **IndiaAI Compute Capacity** — GPU infrastructure & subsidized access
2. **Foundation Models** — Indigenous models (BharatGen, Sarvam AI)
3. **AIKosh (Datasets Platform)** — Open data repository for AI innovation
4. **Application Development** — Innovation challenges, hackathons, sector solutions
5. **FutureSkills** — AI education and workforce skilling
6. **Startup Financing** — Risk capital and deep-tech funding support
7. **Safe & Trusted AI** — Governance frameworks and safety protocols

### Foundation Models
- **BharatGen**: Government-backed consortium led by IIT Bombay; ₹988.6 crore allocation; multimodal models covering 22 scheduled Indian languages
- **Sarvam AI**: Achieved unicorn status (June 2026); $234M Series B led by HCLTech; full-stack sovereign AI for 22 languages
- **Krutrim (Ola)**: Pivoted to domestic AI cloud services stack; first annual net profit FY2026

### AI Startup Ecosystem (2025)
- AI startups raised over **$1.3 billion** across 100+ deals (3x YoY increase)
- Capital flowing into **vertical AI** (legal tech, healthcare, finance)
- Ecosystem transitioning from "research phase" to "deployment phase"

### Sector Applications
- **Healthcare**: AI-assisted diagnostics, early disease detection, personalized treatment
- **Agriculture**: Drone-based crop monitoring, AI weather prediction, WhatsApp-integrated advisory
- **Education**: Personalized multilingual learning platforms, voice-first content delivery
- **Governance**: SabhaSaar (Gram Sabha AI documentation), Sansad Bhashini (parliamentary translation)
- **Financial Services**: RBI FREE-AI framework (August 2025), SEBI AI compliance measures

### Linguistic Infrastructure
- **Bhashini**: Voice-first multilingual AI for 22 scheduled languages + tribal dialects
- **AI4Bharat**: Open-source datasets, Data Management Unit for Bhashini

## AI Governance Framework

### India AI Governance Guidelines (November 2025)
- **Type**: Non-binding, principle-based, "light-touch" risk-based
- **Seven Guiding Sutras**: Trust, People-First, Innovation over Restraint, Fairness & Equity, Safety/Resilience/Sustainability, Accountability, Transparency
- **Three-Tier Institutional Structure**:
  1. AI Governance Group (AIGG) — inter-ministerial policy coordination
  2. Technology & Policy Expert Committee (TPEC) — technical/legal inputs
  3. AI Safety Institute (AISI) — safety testing, risk evaluation, standards

### Regulatory Landscape
- **IT Rules Amendment (February 2026)**: Statutory definition of "synthetically generated information" (SGI); deepfake labeling mandates; 2-3 hour takedown timelines
- **DPDP Act 2023**: Data privacy and algorithmic processing accountability
- **Digital India Act**: Anticipated comprehensive legislation (IT Act 2000 replacement)
- **Sectoral Regulators**: RBI (FREE-AI), SEBI (algorithmic trading AI), sectoral AI compliance
- **Philosophy**: "Voluntary today, mandatory tomorrow" — innovation-permissive but transitioning to binding

### Ethics & Safety Challenges
- Algorithmic bias in credit scoring, hiring, law enforcement
- Deepfake-enabled misinformation and election integrity threats
- "Liar's dividend" problem — bad actors dismissing real content as deepfakes
- Cross-border enforcement difficulties

## Semiconductor & Hardware Independence

### India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)
- **Tata Electronics Dholera Fab**: Partnership with Taiwan PSMC; 28nm–110nm mature nodes; trial production 2027, commercial scaling 2028
- **Semicon 2.0 (July 2026)**: ₹1.27 lakh crore outlay; equipment, materials, advanced packaging, indigenous IP
- **ATMP/OSAT Facilities**: Micron, CG Power, Kaynes in commercial production
- **Total Projects Approved**: 12 semiconductor projects across India

### Geopolitical Positioning
- **Pax Silica Coalition**: US-led supply chain security initiative; India joined
- **Strategic Multi-Alignment**: Western hardware access + global open-weights utilization
- **Data Sovereignty**: DPDP Act + RBI data localization mandates

## Defense & National Security AI

### Institutional Framework
- **DRDO CAIR**: Centre for AI and Robotics — autonomous systems, cybersecurity, AI reconnaissance
- **Defence AI Council (DAIC)** & **Defence AI Project Agency (DAIPA)**
- **iDEX & ADITI**: Innovation programs for defense startups

### Applications
- **AI Kavach**: Predictive cyber threat intelligence
- **Border Security**: Counter-drone tech (D4 Anti-Drone System), intelligent surveillance
- **C4ISR**: Satellite data processing, target detection, deepfake detection
- **Operation Sindoor (May 2025)**: Reported use of AI for multi-sensor data fusion

### Autonomous Weapons Stance
- Maintains "human-in-the-loop" commitment
- Existing IHL sufficient for LAWS regulation (India's CCW position)
