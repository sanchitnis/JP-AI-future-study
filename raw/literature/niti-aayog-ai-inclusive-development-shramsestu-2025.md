# NITI Aayog: AI for Inclusive Societal Development & Digital ShramSetu

**Source Type**: Government Policy Paper & Think Tank Report
**Publishers**: NITI Aayog, PIB, Drishti IAS
**Date**: October 2025

---

## Executive Summary

NITI Aayog released "AI for Inclusive Societal Development" in October 2025 — a landmark study proposing the use of AI, blockchain, and immersive learning technologies to bridge India's digital divide by targeting the needs of India's 490 million informal workers.

---

## Mission Digital ShramSetu

### Vision
A proposed national technology mission acting as a "digital bridge" (ShramSetu — literally "labor bridge") for India's informal economy workforce.

### Target Population
- **490 million informal workers**: Farmers, artisans, construction workers, gig workers, small traders, domestic help, healthcare community workers
- Workers historically excluded from formal digital systems, credit markets, and social security

### Core Components
1. **Verifiable Digital Identities**: AI + blockchain-based identity for workers without formal documentation
2. **Social Security Access**: Automated enrollment and tracking for government welfare schemes
3. **Skill Certifications**: AI-assessed, blockchain-verified portable skill credentials
4. **Fair Compensation**: Smart contract-based wage protection and transparent payment systems
5. **Voice-First AI Tools**: Overcoming literacy barriers through multilingual speech interfaces
6. **Immersive Learning**: AR/VR-based skill training for hands-on vocational education

### Technology Stack
- **AI**: NLP, speech recognition (Bhashini integration), computer vision for skill assessment
- **Blockchain**: Immutable records for identity, credentials, and payment transparency
- **Immersive Tech**: AR/VR for practical skill demonstration in local languages

## #AIforAll Philosophy

### NITI Aayog's Core Principles (since 2021)
1. Safety & Reliability
2. Inclusivity & Non-Discrimination
3. Fairness & Equity
4. Privacy & Data Governance
5. Transparency & Explainability
6. Accountability
7. Protection of Human Values

### Strategic Alignment
- **Viksit Bharat 2047**: AI as instrument for achieving "developed India" status by 2047
- **Human Dignity**: Technology must prioritize equitable opportunity over efficiency alone
- **DPI Integration**: ShramSetu layered on India Stack (Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker)

## Significance for AI in India

### Why This Matters
- Addresses the **"last-mile" AI problem**: Bringing AI benefits beyond urban knowledge workers
- Tests whether India's DPI-first model can extend to the informal economy
- Proposes a **uniquely Indian model** of AI deployment — voice-first, multilingual, welfare-oriented
- Challenges the Silicon Valley narrative that AI primarily serves educated, English-speaking users

### Key Risks
- Digital literacy barriers in target population
- Infrastructure gaps (connectivity, power) in rural areas
- Privacy concerns with biometric and blockchain identity systems
- Implementation coordination across central and state governments
- Quality of AI models for low-resource Indian languages
