# China Ministry of Education: Guidelines for AI Education in Primary and Secondary Schools (2025 Edition)

> **Source**: Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China (MOE). (May 2025).  
> 1. *Guidelines for Artificial Intelligence General Education in Primary and Secondary Schools (2025 Edition)* (中小学人工智能通识教育指南（2025年版）)  
> 2. *Guidelines for the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence in Primary and Secondary Schools (2025 Edition)* (中小学生成式人工智能使用指南（2025年版）)

---

## 1. Context & Strategic Objectives
Promulgated as part of the *Plan for the Construction of China into an Education Powerhouse (2024–2035)* and the *“Artificial Intelligence + Education” Action Plan*, these guidelines establish mandatory national K-12 AI education standards across mainland China, mandating a minimum of 8 hours of structured AI instruction per academic year starting in 2025.

---

## 2. The Four-Dimensional Spiral Curriculum
China structures AI literacy along a developmental "spiral" across four key dimensions: **Cognition (认知)**, **Skills (技能)**, **Thinking (思维)**, and **Values (价值观)**:

1. **Primary School (Grades 1–6)**:
   - Sparks scientific curiosity through tangible robotics, rule-based algorithmic play, and basic coding.
   - **Strict Prohibition**: Independent student use of open-ended generative AI content creation tools is strictly prohibited to prevent cognitive bypassing and protect young neural development.
2. **Junior High School (Grades 7–9)**:
   - Focuses on foundational machine learning concepts: supervised learning, data representation, decision trees, and basic neural network logic.
   - Explores ethical implications: algorithmic bias, deepfake awareness, and intellectual property.
3. **Senior High School (Grades 10–12)**:
   - Focuses on **Systems Thinking** and cross-disciplinary scientific problem-solving.
   - Students design machine learning projects that interface with physics simulations, environmental monitoring, or linguistics.
   - Rigorous study of AI governance, national data security laws, and human-machine collaborative workflows.

---

## 3. Strict Generative AI Classroom Guardrails
- **Anti-Substitution Rule**: Teachers are explicitly barred from using generative AI as a substitute for active instructional responsibilities, personalized student evaluation, or moral character building.
- **Anti-Cognitive Surrender Rule**: Students must never submit AI-generated text or code without verifiable evidence of personal conceptual understanding.
- **Data Sovereignty**: Educational AI applications must use domestic Chinese LLMs (e.g. Baidu ERNIE, Alibaba Tongyi, Zhipu GLM) that comply with China's Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services (2023).
