# Singapore Ministry of Education (MOE): AI-in-Education (AIEd) Framework & Student Learning Space (SLS)

> **Source**: Ministry of Education (MOE), Singapore. (2023–2025). *AI-in-Education (AIEd) Framework, EdTech Masterplan 2030, and Singapore Student Learning Space (SLS) Technical Documentation*.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy
The Singapore Ministry of Education integrates Artificial Intelligence through a centrally governed, human-centric architecture. Rather than allowing uncontrolled access to consumer-facing chatbots, MOE deploys walled-garden AI micro-services inside the national **Singapore Student Learning Space (SLS)** platform, protected by GovTech's *Litmus* and *Sentinel* safety and privacy guardrails.

---

## 2. Four Core Pillars of the AIEd Framework
1. **Agency**: Students and teachers must remain active decision-makers. AI does not dictate learning; it scaffolds inquiry.
2. **Inclusivity**: Ensures high-quality adaptive tutoring is accessible across all socioeconomic profiles, closing rather than widening academic achievement gaps.
3. **Fairness**: Rigorous auditing against linguistic, cultural, and gender bias in pedagogical models.
4. **Safety**: Strict prevention of data exfiltration; identifiable student data is never fed into external commercial LLMs.

---

## 3. The SLS AI Suite: Socratic Tutoring & Teacher Augmentation

### For Students:
- **Learning Assistant (LEA)**: A Socratic-style AI tutor available for Primary 4 and above. By design, LEA **refuses to provide direct answers**. Instead, it breaks complex questions into conceptual sub-steps, prompts the student to identify misconceptions, and provides encouraging hints.
- **Adaptive Learning System (ALS)**: Recommends personalized practice pathways in Mathematics and Geography tailored to individual student mastery zones.
- **Speech Evaluation Tool (SET)**: Real-time vernacular oral feedback for language learning.

### For Teachers:
- **Authoring Copilot (ACP)**: AI-assisted curriculum drafting and rubric generation aligned with Singapore national syllabi.
- **Data Assistant (DAT)**: Semantic clustering of student short answers to surface cohort-wide conceptual misunderstandings in real time.
- **Annotated Feedback Assistant (AFA)** & **Short Answer Feedback Assistant (SAFA)**: Automated formative feedback suggestions that teachers can review, edit, or reject before releasing to students.

---

## 4. Age-Progressive Guardrails
- AI tools are deliberately withheld from early primary students (P1–P3) to safeguard foundational brain development, pencil-and-paper motor skills, and social-emotional rapport.
- Progressive introduction begins in P4 under direct teacher moderation.
