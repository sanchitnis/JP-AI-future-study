# CBSE & NEP 2020: Secondary School AI Curriculum & Capacity Building

> **Source**: Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), Ministry of Education, Government of India. (2023–2026). *National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE) & Artificial Intelligence Secondary Stage Roadmap*.

---

## 1. National Mandate
Under the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 and NCF-SE 2023, Artificial Intelligence and Computational Thinking (CT) are transitioning from elite vocational electives to essential foundational literacies across all secondary schools:
- **Phase 1 (2020–2024)**: AI offered as Skill Subject 417 in Class 9 and 843 in Class 11 in affiliated schools.
- **Phase 2 (2026–2027)**: Computational Thinking and AI integrated across Classes 3 to 8.
- **Phase 3 (2027–2028)**: AI literacy to become an integrated core competency for all students in Classes 9 and 10.

---

## 2. Multidisciplinary Integration
CBSE explicitly directs that AI should not be taught in isolation as mere computer coding. Teachers across Social Science, Science, English, and Mathematics are mandated to integrate AI concepts:
- *Science*: Machine learning models for weather prediction, astronomical image processing, and genetics.
- *Humanities*: Algorithmic bias in historical data, language translation ethics, and data privacy in democracy.
- *Mathematics*: Statistical foundations, matrices, and predictive modeling.

---

## 3. Teacher Professional Development (NISHTHA & DIKSHA)
- Continuous capacity building via NCERT/CIET NISHTHA modules.
- Focus on moving teachers from passive technological consumers to designers of AI-enhanced learning environments.
- High emphasis on student safety, preventing academic malpractice, and fostering ethical awareness around generative media.
