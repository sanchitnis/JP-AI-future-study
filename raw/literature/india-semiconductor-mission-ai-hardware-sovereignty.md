# India Semiconductor Mission & AI Hardware Sovereignty

**Source Type**: Government Policy, Industry Reports, Press Releases
**Publishers**: ISM, MeitY, PIB, NASSCOM
**Date Range**: 2024 – September 2026

---

## Key Facts & Data Points

### India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0
- **Original ISM**: Established 2021 with ₹76,000 crore outlay
- **Semicon 2.0 (July 2026)**: Expanded to ₹1.27 lakh crore; covers equipment, materials, advanced packaging, indigenous IP
- **12 semiconductor projects approved** across India as of mid-2026

### Manufacturing Facilities
- **Tata Electronics Dholera Fab**: Partnership with Taiwan's PSMC; 28nm–110nm mature nodes; trial production ~2027, commercial scaling 2028
- **Micron (Sanand, Gujarat)**: ATMP/OSAT facility — commercial production operational
- **CG Power**: Assembly and testing — commercial production
- **Kaynes**: OSAT facility — commercial production
- **Hub Locations**: Sanand & Dholera (Gujarat) emerging as primary semiconductor corridors

### Strategic Objectives
- **From Import to "Silicon to Systems"**: Building end-to-end semiconductor value chain
- **Mature Nodes Focus**: Initial targeting of automobiles, electronics, industrial systems (not frontier <7nm)
- **Equipment & Materials**: Semicon 2.0 adds upstream semiconductor supply chain
- **Indigenous IP**: Focus on design capabilities, RISC-V architecture potential

## Geopolitical Positioning

### Pax Silica Coalition
- US-led global silicon supply chain security initiative
- India joined alongside Japan, Netherlands, South Korea
- Goal: Diversify from East Asian (Taiwan/TSMC) concentration risk

### Strategic Multi-Alignment
- **Western Hardware Access**: Full integration with Nvidia, AMD, Intel ecosystems
- **Open-Weights Utilization**: Leveraging Chinese open-source models (Qwen, DeepSeek) for cost efficiency
- **Data Sovereignty**: Domestic data processing and storage mandates
- **Strategic Vulnerability**: Dependence on Western chip architectures creates potential supply chain disruption risk

### Comparison with China's Approach
| Factor | India | China |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Silicon Strategy** | Global supply chain integration | Forced domestic substitution (Huawei Ascend) |
| **Driving Force** | Market opportunity & strategic choice | US export sanctions (H100/B200 restrictions) |
| **Node Targeting** | Mature nodes (28nm+) initially | Full spectrum including frontier attempts |
| **Compiler/Software** | Relies on standard CUDA ecosystem | Custom compilers (CANN, MindSpore) |
| **Timeline** | First fab commercial output ~2028 | Huawei Ascend already training frontier models |

## Compute Infrastructure
- **IndiaAI Mission Compute**: 38,000+ GPUs across 15 empaneled providers (as of Sept 2026)
- **Target**: 45,000+ GPUs by end 2026
- **1.1 EFLOPS**: High-Performance AI Compute System at NIC Data Centre, Delhi
- **Hardware Mix**: NVIDIA H100/H200/A100, AMD MI300X, Intel Gaudi 2
- **Subsidy**: Up to 40% cost reduction for eligible startups and researchers
