# UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers (2024)

> **Citation**: UNESCO. (2024). *AI Competency Framework for Teachers (AI CFT)*. United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, Paris. Launched at Digital Learning Week 2024.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Paradigm
The UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers establishes a global standard for preparing educators to navigate generative and predictive AI in primary and secondary schools. The framework explicitly grounds AI integration in human rights, social justice, and teacher agency, transitioning the traditional classroom relationship into a **Teacher–AI–Student Triad** where human pedagogy directs algorithmic affordances.

---

## 2. Five Key Competency Areas (15 Competencies)

### Area 1: Human-Centred Mindset
- Protecting human agency, moral autonomy, and child development against algorithmic determinism.
- Recognizing the limitations of automated systems and ensuring technology never displaces empathetic teacher-student bonds.

### Area 2: Ethics of AI
- Auditing algorithmic bias, gender/cultural stereotyping, and socioeconomic exclusion.
- Enforcing child data protection, digital privacy, intellectual property rights, and environmental sustainability of compute infrastructure.

### Area 3: AI Foundations and Applications
- Understanding underlying architectures: large language models, prompt dynamics, predictive machine learning, and hallucinations.
- Demystifying AI to students, addressing misconceptions and preventing both blind trust and unwarranted technophobia.

### Area 4: AI Pedagogy
- Redesigning instructional design, formative assessment, and classroom activities.
- Moving from passive knowledge delivery to interactive inquiry, problem-based learning, and Socratic dialogue.
- Differentiating instruction for diverse learning paces without stigmatizing struggling learners.

### Area 5: AI for Professional Learning
- Utilizing AI tools to reduce administrative burden (lesson drafting, rubrics, differentiation) so teachers recover time for 1-on-1 student mentorship.
- Participating in collaborative professional learning communities to share vetted prompts and classroom case studies.

---

## 3. Three Progression Levels
1. **Acquire**: Understand AI concepts and operate tools under structured guidance.
2. **Deepen**: Integrate AI into subject-specific pedagogical practice and assessment design.
3. **Create**: Innovate new pedagogical models, mentor peer teachers, and formulate school-level AI ethics policies.
