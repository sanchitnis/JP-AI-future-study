---

## 🎯 What We Reflect On

On a scheduled cadence (quarterly), the study group answers four diagnostic questions:

1. **Where did stages earn their keep?**  
   Did `spec.md` prevent a rewrite, or did it just add bureaucratic delay? Did `review.md` catch verifiable errors, or was it rubber-stamped?
2. **Where is human attention actually going?**  
   Are senior experts still reading drafts start-to-finish, or has their attention successfully shifted to strategic framing, ethical nuance, and high-leverage guidance?
3. **Are certified skills drifting from active standards?**  
   Have policy facts, institutional guidelines, or technology baselines changed since a skill was approved?
4. **Is the loop shape still right for this work?**  
   Do some projects need a lighter 3-stage loop, while others require the full 6-stage machinery?
