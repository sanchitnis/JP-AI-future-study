# Process Reflection: Q1 2026 (Elevating Wiki to AI-Native Project Hub)

> **Cadence**: Inaugural Process Reflection  
> **Audited Framework**: [ai-native-project-playbook.md](../ai-native-project-playbook.md)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Health Check
In Q1 2026, the JP AI Future Study Group completed a fundamental architectural shift: elevating the repository from a passive Second Brain Wiki into an active **AI-Native Study Group Project Hub**. The Wiki (`wiki/`) was repositioned as Component 1 (our persistent Collective Memory Engine), while three high-impact project tracks were launched under `projects/`:
1. `policy-brief-sovereign-ai-compute` (Compute & Infrastructure Track)
2. `curriculum-foundational-ai-literacy` (Education & Human Agency Track)
3. `concept-note-ai-workforce-resilience` (Economy & Labor Track)

---

## 2. Where did stages actually earn their keep?
- **The Spec Step (`spec.md`) Saved Multiple Rewrites**: In the Sovereign Compute brief, locking the non-goals (explicitly excluding chip-level silicon benchmarking) in `spec.md` prevented the agent from generating 5 pages of irrelevant GPU architecture comparisons. The 5 minutes spent locking the spec saved an estimated 2 hours of human review editing.
- **Layered Review in `review.md`**: Dividing the review into Layer 1 (Agent factual grounding & citation check) and Layer 2 (Human domain nuance) allowed domain leads (Saurabh Bodas, Rohan Katepallewar, Abhishek Suryawanshi) to focus solely on high-leverage policy nuance (e.g., qualifying DPDP compliance, startup voucher safeguards) rather than hunting for missing references.

---

## 3. Where is human attention actually going?
- In early drafts, domain experts initially reverted to line-by-line grammar editing. By establishing the principle that "humans supply judgment, direction, and domain facts while agents supply drafting speed," review meetings shifted focus to strategic framing, ethical positioning, and stakeholder reception.
- Total drafting time per deliverable dropped from weeks of stalled committee writing to 48–72 hours of tight human-agent iteration.

---

## 4. Are certified skills drifting from active standards?
- The newly codified skills (`ai-native-project-engine` and `skill-certification-pipeline`) are operating effectively.
- **Next Candidate Skill Identified**: A standardized rubric for "Evaluating Indian Sovereign AI Policy Claims" will be formally drafted and routed to the Compute working track for certification in Q2.

---

## 5. Is the loop shape still right?
- The 6-stage chain (`intent.md` → `spec.md` → `draft.md` → `review.md` → `final.md` → `feedback.md`) is right-sized for policy briefs and curricula.
- For shorter informal session reaction notes, we will permit collapsing `intent.md` and `spec.md` into a single lightweight brief.
- Overall verdict: The AI-native project framework has successfully transformed the study group from passive consumers of weekly webinars into an active research and policy production laboratory.
